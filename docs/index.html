What this is. A Premier League forecast that runs itself. Every club is rated on twelve seasons of results and chance quality, then adjusted for who has signed, who has left, who is injured or suspended and who is now in charge. The remaining fixtures are played out 20,000 times and the finishing positions counted. Nobody presses anything: it republishes twelve hours after each gameweek ends.

Where the data comes from. Expected goals from Understat, results and odds from football-data.co.uk, squads and availability from the official Fantasy Premier League API.
