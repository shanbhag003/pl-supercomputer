----------------------------------------------------
Dixon-Coles xG model, 80 bootstrap refits for parameter uncertainty.
Squad layer: player values from a regularised plus-minus model over
12 seasons, applied to current rosters and availability (FPL).
Data: Understat (xG) + football-data.co.uk (results, odds) + FPL.

Not modelled: manager changes (tested, made forecasts worse, so it
is switched off), European fixture congestion, per-club home advantage.
