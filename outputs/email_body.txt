HOW IT WORKS
----------------------------------------------------------
  1. Every club gets an attack and a defence score, based on twelve
     seasons of results and the quality of chances created.
  2. Those scores are adjusted for who has arrived, who has left,
     who is injured or suspended, and who is now in charge.
  3. Every remaining match is then played out 20,000 times, and we
     count how often each club finished where.

Deliberately left out: fixture congestion (tested across twelve
seasons - the effect was not consistent enough to trust), and manager
changes where the incoming manager has no Premier League record, since
there is nothing to measure them against.
