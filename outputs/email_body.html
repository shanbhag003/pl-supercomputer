How it works
  1. Every club gets an attack and a defence score, from twelve seasons of results and the quality of chances created.
  2. Those scores are adjusted for arrivals, departures, injuries, suspensions and who is now in charge.
  3. Every remaining match is played out 20,000 times, and we count how often each club finished where.
Deliberately left out: fixture congestion (tested across twelve seasons — the effect was not consistent enough to trust), and manager changes where the incoming manager has no Premier League record, since there is nothing to measure them against.
